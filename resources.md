# Resources

This page includes useful resources and tools from other governments, industry organisations as well as notable individuals in the field of accessibility. 

### General resources

* [WCAG quick reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/)
* [Resources listed on The A11y Project](https://www.a11yproject.com/resources/)

* [other bookmarklets from the US government](https://github.com/Section508Coordinators/a11ybookmarklets)
* a [list of tools from 18F](https://accessibility.18f.gov/tools/) (a US government digital department)
* [The German government's list of tools](https://www.bitvtest.de/bitv_test/das_testverfahren_im_detail/werkzeugliste/)
* [DigitaA11y's list of bookmarklets](https://www.digitala11y.com/accessibility-bookmarklets-testing/)
* [Level Access' bookmarklets](https://labs.levelaccess.com/index.php/Category:Favlet)
* [TPG's bookmarklets](https://github.com/ThePacielloGroup/bookmarklets)
* [Adrian Roselli's bookmarklets](https://adrianroselli.com/2015/01/css-bookmarklets-for-testing-and-fixing.html)
* [Lloydi's A11y Tools](https://a11y-tools.com/), including bookmarklets

### Other testing instructions

* The [Home Office's testing instructions](https://design.homeoffice.gov.uk/accessibility/standard)
* The German government's [old test list](https://testen.bitv-test.de/index.php?a=dl&mode=wcag) and [new test list](https://webtest.bitv-test.de/index.php?a=dl&t=s) (the new one covers WCAG only under 9.x points)
* The [US government Trusted Tester programme](https://www.dhs.gov/trusted-tester) - this includes a [checklist for Section 508](https://section508coordinators.github.io/TrustedTester/) (which is equivalent to WCAG 2.0, so the new 2.1 criteria are missing)
* The [Swedish government's instructions](https://trg.digg.se/manual/)
* The [Latvian government's instructions](https://pieklustamiba.varam.gov.lv/)
* [Microsoft's Accessibility Insights](https://accessibilityinsights.io/) checklist
* [IBM Accessibility Requirements](https://www.ibm.com/able/requirements/requirements/)
* [Orange's testing instructions](https://a11y-guidelines.orange.com/en/)
* [W3C's ​​Accessibility Education and Outreach Working Group's checklist](https://www.w3.org/WAI/EO/wiki/Accessibility_Checkpoint_Master_List)
